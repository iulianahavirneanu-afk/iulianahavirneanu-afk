# Academic Profile

I am a PhD student at **Ștefan cel Mare University of Suceava (USV)**, Romania, focusing on Computational Linguistics, Natural Language Processing, and Artificial Intelligence.

### 🔬 Doctoral Research

My doctoral research explores computational methods for linguistic analysis, with a particular focus on **AI-generated text detection** and the investigation of linguistic patterns associated with large language models.

### 🧠 Research Interests

- Computational Linguistics & Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- AI-Generated Text Detection
- Human-Written vs. AI-Generated Text Analysis
- Linguistic and Stylometric Analysis

### 📜 Research Principles

- **Open Science** & **Reproducible Research**
- **Research Integrity** & **Responsible Artificial Intelligence**
